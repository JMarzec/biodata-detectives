# BioData Detectives: Cancer & Nutrition Lab
## User Guide & Quick Start

---

## 🎮 **What is BioData Detectives?**

BioData Detectives is an interactive science quiz game designed for the NOVA Medical School Open Day. Players compete in teams to solve real-world biomedical data challenges inspired by transcriptomics research—the study of how genes are expressed in disease and health.

The game teaches 17-year-olds (and anyone interested in Medicine or Nutrition) about:
- **Gene expression patterns** and how they reveal disease mechanisms
- **Critical thinking** in interpreting scientific data
- **Biomarkers** and their role in diagnosis and treatment
- **Nutrition and metabolism** in cancer biology

**No login required. No personal data collected. Completely anonymous.**

---

## 🚀 **How to Access the Game**

### **For Players:**
1. **Scan the QR code** displayed at the stand/booth
2. Or visit the game URL directly on your phone
3. Choose your game mode:
   - **Create Your Team** (Normal Mode) - 30-second timer per question
   - **Expert Mode** (⚡ Harder Questions, Faster Timer) - 15-second timer, 1.5x score multiplier
   - **Play Without Team** - Quick play without team name
   - **Create Team Session** (Multi-Player) - Create a session and share join code with teammates
   - **Join Team** (Multi-Player) - Join an existing team using a 6-character code
   - **Leaderboard** - View live rankings

### **For Staff/Organizers:**
1. Access the **Admin Panel** at `/admin`
2. View the **QR code** to display on projector
3. Monitor **live leaderboard** and player statistics
4. Access **controls** for demo mode, CSV export, and leaderboard reset

## 📋 **Game Flow**

### **Step 1: Welcome Screen**
- Choose your **language** (English or Portuguese)
- Select your **game mode**:
  - **Create Your Team** - Enter a team name (or get a random science-themed name)
  - **Expert Mode** - Same as above but with harder questions and faster timer
  - **Play Without Team** - Quick play without team setup
  - **Create Team Session** (Multi-Player) - Create a session and share join code
  - **Join Team** (Multi-Player) - Join using a 6-character code
  - **Leaderboard** - View top scores

### **Step 2: Team Setup** (if creating a team)
- Enter your **team name** or choose a random suggestion
- Random name generator offers fun science-themed options like:
  - RNA Rangers
  - Gene Ninjas
  - Protein Pioneers
  - Mutation Masters
  - Biomarker Hunters
- Click **"Start Mission"** to proceed

### **Step 3: Mission Briefing**
- Read the **educational introduction** about transcriptomics and critical thinking
- **30-second countdown** enforces reading time
- Click **"Start Game"** when ready

### **Step 4: Gameplay - 3 Rounds**
Each round presents 3 questions (9 total) from different categories:

#### **Round 1: RNA Detective** 🧬
- Analyze gene expression patterns
- Interpret treatment response signals
- Questions about cancer biology and RNA levels

#### **Round 2: Nutrition & Cell Stress** 🥗
- Explore metabolism and inflammation
- Understand how nutrition affects cellular signals
- Analyze stress response markers

#### **Round 3: Biomarker Mission** 🔬
- Critical thinking about validation and evidence
- Interpret complex biomarker patterns
- Apply knowledge to real-world scenarios

### **Step 5: Each Question**
1. **Read the scenario** - Fictional patient case with molecular data
2. **View signal bars** - Visual representation of gene expression levels (low/medium/high)
3. **Choose your answer** - Select from 2-4 options
4. **Submit** - Click "Submit Answer"
5. **Get feedback** - See if you're correct with scientific explanation
6. **Continue** - Click "Next Question" to proceed

### **Step 6: Results Screen**
- View your **final score**
- See your **accuracy percentage**
- Check your **rank** based on performance
- Options to:
  - **Play Again** - Return to welcome screen
  - **View Leaderboard** - See all team rankings

## 🏆 **Scoring System**

### **Points Calculation:**
- **Correct answer**: 100 points
- **Speed bonus**: +20 points (if answered in under 30 seconds)
- **Incorrect answer**: 0 points

### **Example:**
- 3 correct answers, all under 30s = 360 points
- 2 correct + 1 incorrect, all under 30s = 240 points
- 3 correct, 1 over 30s, 2 under 30s = 340 points

### **Expert Mode Multiplier:**
- All scores × 1.5 (50% bonus)
- Faster timer: 15 seconds instead of 30 seconds
- Same scoring rules apply

### **Ranking System:**
Based on accuracy percentage (correct answers ÷ total questions):
1. **Senior BioData Investigators** - 90%+ accuracy
2. **Principal Biomedical Analysts** - 75-89% accuracy
3. **Biomedical Data Analysts** - 60-74% accuracy
4. **Data Explorers** - 45-59% accuracy
5. **Researchers in Training** - Below 45% accuracy

## ❓ **Frequently Asked Questions**

### **Q: Can I play without creating a team?**
A: Yes! Click "Play Without Team" to skip team setup and start playing immediately.

### **Q: Is my data saved?**
A: No personal data is collected. Only your team name and score are stored on the leaderboard. No login or personal information required.

### **Q: Can I play multiple times?**
A: Yes! After finishing, click "Play Again" to return to the welcome screen and start a new game.

### **Q: What if I run out of time on a question?**
A: You must submit an answer before moving to the next question. The timer is just for scoring the speed bonus—it doesn't force submission.

### **Q: How is the leaderboard sorted?**
A: By total score (highest first). Ties are broken by accuracy percentage, then by time taken.

### **Q: Can I see my individual answers?**
A: The results screen shows your final score, accuracy, and rank. Detailed answer review can be added in future versions.

### **Q: Is Expert Mode really harder?**
A: Yes! Expert questions have more complex scenarios, ambiguous signals, and require deeper analysis. The 15-second timer also adds pressure.

### **Q: Can teams collaborate on one phone?**
A: Yes! Multiple people can discuss and decide on answers together using one phone. This is encouraged for team learning.

## 👥 **Multi-Player Team Joining (NEW!)**

### **What is Team Joining?**

Team Joining allows multiple players on different phones to play together as a single team. One player creates a session and shares a 6-character join code with teammates. All team members see each other in the lobby and can start the game together.

### **How to Create a Team Session:**

1. Click **"👥 Create Team"** button on the welcome screen
2. Enter your **team name** (or use a random suggestion)
3. Choose your **difficulty** (Normal or Expert Mode)
4. A unique **6-character join code** is generated (e.g., `ABC123`)
5. **Share the code** with your teammates (copy button available)
6. Teammates join using the code (see below)
7. When all teammates are ready, click **"Start Game"**
8. All team members play together and their scores are combined

### **How to Join a Team Session:**

1. Click **"🔗 Join Team"** button on the welcome screen
2. Enter the **6-character join code** provided by your team leader
3. You'll see the **Team Lobby** with all joined members
4. Wait for the team leader to start the game
5. When the game starts, you'll play the same questions as your teammates
6. Your individual score contributes to the team's final score

### **Team Joining Features:**

- **Real-time member list**: See who has joined your team
- **Member status tracking**: Know who is playing, completed, or disconnected
- **Synchronized game start**: All team members start at the same time
- **Shared session**: One session code works for up to 10 team members
- **Session expiration**: Sessions expire after 1 hour of inactivity
- **Bilingual support**: Create and join sessions in English or Portuguese

### **Tips for Team Play:**

- **Discuss answers**: Talk with your teammates before answering
- **Divide and conquer**: Assign different questions to different members
- **Speed matters**: Faster answers earn bonus points
- **Expert Mode challenge**: Try Expert Mode for harder questions and faster timer
- **Share the code**: Use chat, QR code, or verbal communication to share the join code
